Delhi Metro Route Planner

Delhi Metro Route Planner helps commuters find practical metro routes across Delhi NCR before opening the interactive map. Search by source and destination station to check the recommended route, estimated fare, travel time, stop count, interchange stations, and line-color guidance. The planner is useful for daily office travel, airport trips, college commutes, shopping journeys, and quick station-to-station route checks.

Popular route examples include Rajiv Chowk to Kashmere Gate, New Delhi to Airport T-3, Hauz Khas to Botanical Garden, and Dwarka Sector 21 to Rithala. You can also browse important station pages such as Rajiv Chowk, Kashmere Gate, New Delhi, and Netaji Subhash Place.

The app supports clean route pages for search engines and shareable planner URLs for users. Each route page opens with the source and destination already selected, then loads the same interactive planner used on the homepage. For official network notices and passenger information, visit the Delhi Metro Rail Corporation.

Use the planner when you want a quick answer before leaving home, changing lines, or comparing two possible metro journeys. The route result focuses on the details commuters usually need first: where the journey starts, where it ends, how many stations are involved, whether a line change is likely, and how long the trip may take. Because every important route has its own page, you can bookmark a regular commute, share it with someone else, or open the same journey again without manually selecting stations each time.

The planner is also built for discovery. Station pages explain connected stations and link to useful routes, while route pages describe the complete journey between two metro stations. This structure helps commuters find answers through search and then continue directly into the interactive experience. Whether you are checking a short neighboring-station trip or a longer journey across multiple Delhi Metro lines, the page gives you a starting estimate before you confirm details for the day of travel.

About Delhi Metro Route Planner

Delhi Metro Route Planner is an independent commuter tool created by Pratik Sharma. It helps users search Delhi Metro station-to-station routes, estimate fares, compare travel time, count stops, and identify possible interchange stations before opening the interactive map.

The site includes static pages for every ordered Delhi Metro station pair, station pages for the network, a sitemap index for crawlers, and an llms.txt file for AI search engines. The planner is not an official Delhi Metro Rail Corporation service; official service notices should be checked with DMRC.
