Delhi Metro Route Planner

Long description:
Delhi Metro Route Planner helps commuters quickly plan routes across the Delhi Metro network. Choose a source and destination station to see the recommended route, estimated fare, travel time, stop count, interchange stations, and line-color guidance on an interactive map. The app works in the browser, supports installable PWA behavior, and includes route-specific pages for station-to-station journeys.
